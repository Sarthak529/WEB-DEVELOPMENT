Monkey D. Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy is the main protagonist of the One Piece series. He is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and has the ability to stretch his body like rubber after eating a Devil Fruit called the Gomu Gomu no Mi. Luffy's dream is to find the legendary One Piece treasure and become the Pirate King. He is known for his carefree and adventurous personality, as well as his strong sense of justice and loyalty to his friends.
